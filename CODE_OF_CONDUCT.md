# Code of conduct

## Our standard

We want participation in ha-didcomm to be safe, constructive, and welcoming.
Contributors must treat others with respect, assume good faith, accept
technical disagreement without personal attacks, and make space for people
with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Examples of welcome behavior include:

- giving specific, actionable, and kind feedback;
- acknowledging mistakes and correcting them;
- respecting privacy and responsible-disclosure boundaries; and
- focusing discussion on what is best for users and the project.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, deliberate
intimidation, and sustained disruption are not acceptable. Publishing another
person's private information or security report without permission is also
not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies in project repositories, discussions, issue trackers,
reviews, and other spaces where someone represents the project.

Report conduct concerns through a private contact method listed on the
maintainer's GitHub profile. If no private method is available, open an issue
asking for a private channel without including sensitive details. Reports will
be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as practical. Project maintainers
may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently exclude
participants whose behavior violates this policy. Good-faith reports will not
result in retaliation.

The maintainer is responsible for applying this policy fairly and may publish
an anonymized explanation of an enforcement decision when doing so helps the
community understand the standard.
